## Day 5 — The Fruit of the Tree

> *Love turns even the instrument of death into a tree of life.*

Truth does not always enter the world gently. When light exposes what has been hidden, the structures built in darkness may resist it. Christ came revealing the Father, and the powers of fear and forgetfulness could not receive what he carried, because his presence threatened the world they had constructed.

So they took him and nailed him to a tree. They intended the cross to silence him, to make weakness of his strength and shame of his life. Yet the very place meant for his destruction became the place where the knowledge of the Father was offered to everyone.

This is a strange image: Christ becoming fruit upon the tree. The cross, an instrument designed to produce suffering and death, bears something nourishing instead. Those who reach toward this fruit do not receive condemnation; they taste the love of the Father and discover life growing where death seemed to have won.

Perhaps there is something here for the wounded places within you. You may carry memories of things that should never have happened, seasons when something precious was lost, or moments when another person's actions left a mark you did not choose. Faith does not require you to call these things good simply because God can meet you within them.

The cross does not teach us that suffering is secretly beautiful. It reveals that suffering does not have the final authority to name what something will become. God can enter even the places shaped by cruelty, rejection, and loss and bring forth something that those forces never intended.

This is the quiet mystery of transformation. A wound can become a place of compassion without ceasing to have been a wound. A failure can become wisdom without becoming necessary, and grief can deepen love without making the loss itself good.

Christ does not destroy those who come to the tree hungry. He feeds them. What appeared to be judgment becomes discovery, and what seemed to be an ending becomes an invitation to know the Father more deeply.

There may be places in your own life that still look only like crosses. You do not need to rush to explain them or force them into a story with a tidy ending. Simply leave room for the possibility that love is still at work there, quietly bringing forth fruit from a tree that once seemed capable only of death.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Meet me in my wounds.

> **Exhale:** Bring forth life.
