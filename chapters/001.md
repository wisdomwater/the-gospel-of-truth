## Day 1 — The Joy of Knowing

> *You were known before you began to seek.*

There is a joy that comes when you discover the One you have been seeking. Perhaps you have felt the longing without knowing its name, a quiet restlessness beneath all the other desires that fill your days. You have been looking for home without realizing that home has been holding you all along.

You may imagine God as distant, somewhere beyond you, waiting at the end of a long spiritual journey. So you search through beliefs and practices, through prayer and silence, through certainty and doubt, hoping eventually to find the path that leads there. Yet the distance you feel is not the deepest truth about you.

You have come from the Fullness of God, from a life greater than anything your mind can contain. Before you learned the names by which you call yourself, before you succeeded or failed, before you knew enough to search for God, you were already held within the thought and love of the Father. Nothing about you began outside that embrace.

This is why the Word comes to us. Christ enters our forgetfulness to make visible what has become hidden, awakening within us the knowledge of the Father. He does not merely bring information about God; he reminds us where we came from and turns our faces again toward home.

Much of your life may be spent trying to satisfy this ancient longing without recognizing it. You may seek accomplishment, possessions, approval, relationships, experiences, or even religious certainty, hoping one of them will finally quiet the restlessness within you. These things may carry genuine goodness, but none of them can bear the full weight of your longing.

There is something within you that remembers its Source. Even your searching can become a kind of memory, a faint recognition that you belong to something larger than the small world you have constructed around yourself. The ache itself can become an invitation to turn toward the One from whom you came.

Do not worry if you cannot understand this One completely. The Father is beyond every image you form and greater than every thought you think. You were never meant to contain the mystery of God, only to awaken within it.

And so begin with joy. You are not wandering through an empty universe trying to find a God who has disappeared. You are learning to recognize the One who has been nearer than your own breath, discovering that the journey toward God is also a journey home.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** I come from You.

> **Exhale:** Lead me home.
