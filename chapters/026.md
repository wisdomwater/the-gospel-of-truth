## Day 26 — The Physician Comes

> *Grace moves toward the place that hurts.*

To turn back is to repent, but repentance need not begin in shame. It is the simple movement of turning again toward life when you recognize that you have wandered from it. You do not return because the Father is waiting to punish you, but because something within you remembers where rest can be found.

Think of God as a physician who hurries toward the place of sickness. A good physician is not offended by the wound or disgusted by the weakness of the one who needs care. The wound itself becomes the reason to draw near, because healing naturally moves toward what is hurting.

You may be tempted to hide what feels incomplete in you. Shame teaches you to cover your wounds until you can make yourself presentable, as though healing were a reward given to those who no longer need it. But you do not need to become well before allowing the physician to enter the room.

The Father possesses what you lack, not as something held over you, but as fullness eager to give itself. Where you are empty, grace pours itself out; where you are weary, rest draws near. Divine abundance does not shame your deficiency but quietly fills it.

This changes the way you can look at the unfinished places within yourself. Your weakness does not have to become a verdict about your character, and your failures do not need to become names by which you call yourself. They can become places where you learn to receive what you cannot manufacture on your own.

Forgiveness is part of this healing. It does not pretend that wounds were never made or that choices carry no consequences. It releases you from remaining forever inside the moment of failure, allowing light to reach what shame would rather keep hidden.

Perhaps repentance, then, is less like crawling back to an angry judge and more like opening the door when the physician arrives. You allow yourself to be seen as you are and trust that the One who sees the wound also knows how to tend it. What you bring is your need; what the Father brings is fullness.

So do not hide the places that still ache. Let grace move toward them, and allow the light of truth to shine where you have feared being seen. The physician has come not because you are already whole, but because love desires to make you whole.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Come to what is wounded.

> **Exhale:** Fill me with Your grace.
