## Day 12 — The Father’s Open Heart

> *The heart of God is open toward you.*

The Word comes forth from the heart of the Father, carrying what has always been hidden there. Wisdom moves through him, love takes form around him, and joy follows in his presence. Christ is not trying to persuade a reluctant God to love you; he is revealing the love that was already there.

This changes the way you may have learned to imagine God. Perhaps you have pictured the Father with folded arms, waiting for you to become worthy enough to approach. You may have thought of Christ as standing between you and divine anger, convincing God to show mercy when judgment would otherwise have prevailed.

But look again at the heart being revealed. The Father opens his own embrace, and the Spirit comes forth from that openness. Christ leads you toward God because he has come from God, carrying the tenderness of the Father into every place where fear has taught you to hide.

The Word does not come merely to speak. He enters the shape of human life, meeting you where you are and gathering what has become scattered. He cleanses what has been clouded by forgetfulness and gently turns you toward the Source from which you came.

You may have spent years searching for the Father without realizing that the Father was already reaching toward you. Spiritual striving can become exhausting when you believe everything depends upon finding the correct path, achieving the proper state of mind, or making yourself acceptable. Eventually the soul grows weary of trying to earn what love has been offering freely.

There comes a time when searching must give way to being found. You begin to recognize that the longing within you has been met by a deeper longing, the desire of the Father to make himself known. What seemed like your solitary journey toward God was held all along within God's movement toward you.

This is where rest begins. Rest is not the end of growth or the absence of questions; it is the end of believing that you must struggle your way into the Father's heart. You discover that the heart you were trying to enter has already opened toward you.

Let yourself remain there for a while. You do not need to explain the mystery or hurry toward the next spiritual insight. Receive the love revealed in Christ, breathe in the Spirit of the open heart, and allow your weary searching to become rest.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Your heart is open.

> **Exhale:** I rest in Your love.
