## Day 2 — The Fog of Ignorance

> *Fear grows in the places where we have forgotten love.*

When you lose sight of the Father, fear begins to fill the empty spaces. What was once clear becomes uncertain, and what was once near begins to feel distant. Like a fog settling over familiar ground, forgetfulness can make you feel lost even when you have never truly left home.

Fear has a way of making itself substantial. It whispers that you are alone, that you must protect yourself, that everything depends upon your ability to understand and control what happens next. The longer you live inside that fear, the more solid it can seem, until you begin arranging your life around something that was born from forgetting.

From this fog comes error. You begin constructing versions of reality that seem convincing because you cannot see beyond them. You imagine God as distant or angry, yourself as abandoned or unworthy, and the world as a place where you must struggle constantly to secure your place.

These thoughts can become beautiful and elaborate. You can build entire systems around them, defending them because they have helped you make sense of what you could not see. Yet something can appear powerful and still not be true, just as shadows can seem threatening when the room is dark.

Do not be ashamed of the fog. You have known moments when fear narrowed your vision and uncertainty made you reach for whatever explanation seemed available. Compassion begins when you recognize that much of what you call error may simply be the mind trying to find its way through darkness.

But the fog does not change what lies beneath it. The landscape remains even when you cannot see it, and the sun has not disappeared because clouds have covered the sky. In the same way, the Father remains unchanged beneath every fear, every confusion, and every mistaken image you have carried.

Truth does not need to fight for its existence. It rests quietly beneath the stories fear has taught you to believe, patient and undisturbed. You may forget the Father, but the Father does not forget you.

So when fear becomes dense and you cannot see the way forward, resist the temptation to believe that the fog is all there is. Let yourself become still enough to remember what fear has hidden from view. Beneath everything that shifts and trembles, there is a Love that remains unchanging, unperturbed, and completely beautiful.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** When I cannot see,

> **Exhale:** Your love remains.
