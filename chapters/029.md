## Day 29 — The Name

> *The name of the unseen Father is spoken in the Son.*

You cannot name what lies entirely beyond your knowing. Words depend upon boundaries, upon distinguishing one thing from another, yet the Father exceeds every boundary you can draw. Whatever name you give to God points toward the mystery, but no word can contain the One from whom all words arise.

And yet the Father desires to be known. The invisible does not remain forever hidden in silence but gives himself a name, not a name borrowed from human language, but a living name arising from his own depths. That name is the Son.

Christ makes the unseen Father visible. If you wonder what divine goodness looks like when it enters the world, look toward the One who touches the rejected, welcomes the forgotten, forgives enemies, restores the fallen, and draws near to those who suffer. The name of God becomes something you can encounter in a human life.

This is more than attaching the correct word to God. A name carries presence and relationship; to know someone's name is to move from abstraction toward encounter. In Christ, the Father does not give you a definition to master but a life through which divine love can be known.

Perhaps this is why your images of God matter so deeply. If the name you carry for God is fear, you may spend your life hiding; if it is judgment, you may learn to judge yourself and others. But if the name revealed in the Son is compassion, goodness, and love, then knowing God slowly changes the way you inhabit the world.

There will always be mystery beyond what has been revealed. The Father remains greater than every image, every doctrine, and even every name your mind can hold. Yet mystery need not mean distance, because the One beyond understanding has chosen to draw near.

And what is revealed is sweetness. Beneath all your questions about God is the invitation to encounter a goodness that does not need to be defended from you. Christ comes from the depths of the Father to tell you that the place from which you came and the place toward which you are returning is held within love.

So let the name of the Son reshape the name you carry for God. Allow every image built from fear to be tested against the love revealed in Christ. The Father may remain beyond all your words, but his heart has not remained hidden.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Reveal Your name in me.

> **Exhale:** Let me know Your love.
