## Day 3 — When Forgetfulness Ends

> *What has no root cannot remain forever.*

Do not give too much power to the things that have grown from fear. Error can seem enormous when you are living inside it, filling your imagination with worries, accusations, and stories about who you are and what the world means. Yet much of its strength comes from your believing that it is permanent.

Forgetfulness has no root of its own. It exists only because something has slipped from your awareness, like darkness filling a room when the light is hidden. You do not need to wrestle darkness to the ground; you need only uncover what has been there all along.

This is why knowledge of the Father changes everything. To know God is not merely to collect better ideas or replace one set of beliefs with another. It is to awaken again to the Presence beneath your life, the Love from which you came and within which you have never ceased to exist.

When you forget this, other things rush in to fill the space. Fear tells you that you are alone, shame tells you that you are unwanted, and anxiety tells you that everything depends upon you. These voices may become familiar enough that you mistake them for truth, but familiarity does not give them roots.

You may even discover that some fears have shaped you for years. They have influenced the way you see yourself, the way you imagine God, and the way you move through the world. Do not condemn yourself for having lived beneath their shadow; simply begin to notice what happens when light reaches them.

Knowledge does not arrive as an accusation. It comes as remembrance. Something within you recognizes the Father again, and what once seemed immovable begins quietly to lose its hold.

This is how forgetfulness ends. Not through violence against yourself, nor through frantic attempts to become worthy of God, but through awakening to what is already true. The Father has not been absent during the years you were searching, and your confusion has never been greater than the Love surrounding it.

Let that knowledge enter the places where fear has made its home. You do not have to solve every uncertainty today or untangle every mistaken belief you have ever carried. Turn toward the light you can see, and allow what has no root to fade in the presence of what has always been real.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Awaken me to Your presence.

> **Exhale:** Let forgetfulness fade.
