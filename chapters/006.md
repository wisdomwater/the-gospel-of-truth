## Day 6 — What the All Lacked

> *What you lack is not God’s love, but the knowing of it.*

You are held within a mystery larger than you can understand. The Father contains all things, yet cannot be contained by anything, and your life has never existed apart from this divine Fullness. Even when you feel distant from God, you remain surrounded by the One in whom all things live.

The strange ache within you comes from not fully knowing what is already yours. You may feel incomplete and assume that something essential has been withheld, as though God possesses a fullness that you must somehow persuade him to share. But the Father is not jealous of his goodness, nor does he guard his life from you.

God desires to be known and loved. The fullness you seek is not a prize reserved for those who become sufficiently spiritual, nor is it a reward for finally getting everything right. It is given as a way of return, an invitation to discover more deeply the One in whom your life has always been held.

Still, you cannot possess the fullness of God. The Father remains greater than every experience, doctrine, image, or revelation through which you encounter him. There will always be depths beyond the depths you have known, because the infinite cannot be exhausted by the finite.

This need not leave you frustrated. A cup does not need to contain the ocean in order to be filled by it. You can receive what is given without needing to master the mystery from which it comes, allowing knowledge to become communion rather than possession.

Perhaps much of your restlessness comes from searching for something you believe is missing. You look for another achievement, another experience, another certainty that might finally make you whole. Yet beneath these desires may be a simpler hunger: to know that you belong to the Father and to live from that belonging.

This is the knowledge that begins to heal your sense of deficiency. You discover that God has not been withholding himself from you, and that the distance you feared was never the deepest reality. The One you seek has been drawing you toward himself through the very longing that made you search.

Let yourself rest for a moment in that possibility. You do not have to grasp the whole mystery, and you do not have to become something else before you can begin receiving it. What the heart has lacked is the knowledge of the Father, and the Father is already making himself known.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** You hold all things.

> **Exhale:** Let me rest in You.
