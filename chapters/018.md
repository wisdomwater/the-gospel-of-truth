## Day 18 — Awakening

> *Grace does not shout you awake; it takes you by the hand.*

There comes a moment when the dream begins to loosen its hold. What seemed unquestionable in the darkness looks different in the morning light, and you begin to recognize how much of your life was shaped by what you could not yet see. Awakening is not an accusation against the person you were; it is the gift of seeing more clearly now.

Perhaps you can look back and recognize earlier versions of yourself who lived inside fears you no longer carry. You made choices from the understanding available to you, sometimes stumbling because you could not see another way. There is little kindness in judging yesterday's self by the light you have only received today.

The Spirit approaches differently. When you are lying on the ground, it does not stand above you demanding that you rise. It bends toward you, reaches out a hand, and helps you find your feet.

This is the tenderness of awakening. You are not dragged from ignorance through shame, nor punished for having been asleep. You are raised, steadied, and given the strength to stand in a world that is slowly becoming visible.

Then something beautiful happens: knowing God becomes more than an idea. You are invited to see, hear, taste, smell, and touch the beloved Son, as though every sense were being awakened to divine presence. Truth is no longer merely something you agree with; it becomes something you encounter.

Perhaps this is why spiritual awakening can happen through ordinary things. A piece of music opens something in you, sunlight falls through a window, someone offers unexpected kindness, or silence becomes strangely full. For a moment you are not thinking about God so much as sensing that you are standing within God.

Do not rush these moments or try to turn them immediately into explanations. Let yourself receive what is being given. The mind has its place, but some knowledge enters first through wonder, beauty, tenderness, and the quiet recognition of presence.

You are waking, and you do not have to awaken all at once. Let the Spirit take your hand whenever you find yourself on the ground, and allow yourself to be raised again. Open your eyes to the light that is already here, and receive the beloved One with every part of your life.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Take me by the hand.

> **Exhale:** Awaken me to You.
