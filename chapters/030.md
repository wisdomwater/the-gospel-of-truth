## Day 30 — Returning to the Father

> *You are returning to the Love from which you came.*

There is a place from which your life has come, and something within you remembers it. You have tasted its goodness in fragments: in moments of love, beauty, belonging, silence, and unexpected peace. These glimpses awaken a longing for the Fullness from which they came.

Your roots reach deeper than the story you can remember. Beneath your accomplishments and failures, beneath the identities you have gathered and the wounds you have carried, your life is rooted in the Father. Like a tree reaching toward the sun while drawing life from hidden soil, you are being drawn toward the Source that has sustained you all along.

This is where the long search leads: into rest. You do not arrive by finally mastering every mystery or answering every question. You arrive by discovering that truth is not merely something you have been searching for; it is something in which you are invited to live.

And the Father you find there is not small, bitter, or angry. The One waiting at the heart of reality is good, unperturbed, and sweet, knowing you before you knew yourself. Much of what fear taught you about God can finally fall away in the presence of this goodness.

Here envy and striving begin to loosen their hold. There is nothing you need to take from another person because the life of the Father is not scarce. You do not have to compete for belonging or prove that you deserve your place; you can simply receive the fullness that has always been offered.

Perhaps this is what salvation has been moving toward from the beginning. Not escape from the world, but awakening within it; not the rejection of yourself, but the recovery of your deepest self in God. The Father is in you, and you are in the Father, held within a communion that neither death nor forgetfulness can finally destroy.

So let yourself rest from the endless search. There will always be more to discover, but discovery no longer needs to arise from anxiety. You can seek from within belonging, wonder from within love, and walk into mystery knowing that your roots remain secure.

You began with joy, with the discovery that the One you were seeking had been nearer than you imagined. Now the journey ends where it began, in the heart of the Father, where nothing essential is lacking and the Spirit gives rest. You are a child of this Love, and you are loved.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** I come from Your love.

> **Exhale:** I return to Your love.
