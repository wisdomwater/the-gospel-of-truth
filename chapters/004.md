## Day 4 — A Path Through Darkness

> *Truth becomes a path when you begin to walk in it.*

There are seasons when forgetfulness feels like darkness. You may still speak the language of faith and carry familiar beliefs, yet something within you cannot see clearly. God seems hidden, the way forward uncertain, and even your own heart can become difficult to understand.

Christ enters this darkness not merely to tell you that light exists, but to illuminate the ground beneath your feet. He comes into the places where you have forgotten who God is and who you are, revealing the Father through mercy. What was hidden begins to emerge, not all at once, but with enough light for another step.

You may wish for something more dramatic. You may want every question answered, every doubt resolved, and the whole road illuminated before you begin. Yet often the light of God comes more gently, showing you what is needed for this moment and inviting you to trust what you cannot yet see.

The path Christ reveals is truth, but truth is more than a collection of correct ideas. It is a way of seeing and a way of living, a gradual alignment of your life with what is deepest and most real. As you walk in truth, the illusions formed in darkness begin to lose their power over you.

Sometimes truth will feel like recognition. You hear something that reaches beneath your defenses, and some quiet place within you whispers, *Yes, this is the way.* You recognize mercy as more trustworthy than fear, love as deeper than condemnation, and the presence of God as nearer than the distance you imagined.

Do not be troubled when you cannot see very far ahead. A path does not require you to know every turn before you take the first step. It asks only that you remain attentive to the light you have been given and allow it to lead you farther into what is true.

Christ does not stand at the end of the road waiting for you to find him. He meets you within the darkness and becomes the way through it. Every step toward truth becomes a step toward the Father, and every movement toward the Father brings you more fully into the light.

So walk gently with the light you have today. You do not need to force certainty where mystery remains, nor fear the shadows that still linger beyond your sight. The One who illuminates the path is already walking it with you.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Light my path.

> **Exhale:** Lead me in truth.
