## Day 22 — Awaken Those Who Sleep

> *What has awakened in you can become light for another.*

There comes a time when the path turns outward. You have been learning to recognize the Father, to awaken from fear, and to receive the hand that lifts you when you fall. Now the life growing within you begins naturally to move toward others.

Speak truth to those who are searching, but do not use truth as a weapon. Offer what you have discovered as bread rather than as a test someone must pass. The person before you may not need an argument nearly as much as they need a glimpse of the love that helped you see differently.

When someone stumbles, help them find their footing. When someone is weak, reach out your hand; when someone is hungry, offer nourishment; when a heart is troubled, make room for it to rest. The knowledge of the Father becomes visible when what you have received begins taking the form of mercy.

You do not have to rescue everyone you meet. Some burdens are not yours to carry, and love does not require you to have an answer for every sorrow. Often the simplest gift is presence: refusing to turn away from another person when their pain makes you uncomfortable.

Encourage those who love, because even loving people grow tired. There are souls quietly doing good who wonder whether their kindness matters, whether anyone notices, or whether it would be easier to harden themselves. Sometimes a small word of tenderness can help someone remain open when they are tempted to close.

And awaken those who sleep, not by shaking them violently, but by becoming a presence of morning. Remember how gently the Spirit took you by the hand when you were on the ground. Extend that same gentleness to those who are still living inside dreams you have begun to leave behind.

Perhaps this is what spiritual maturity looks like. The knowledge you have received becomes encouragement, and the light that awakened you begins helping others see. You stop asking only what God can give you and begin noticing where the life of God might move through you.

Let your life become an invitation rather than a demand. Speak when words bring life, reach when someone needs a hand, nourish what is hungry, and make space for what is weary. The Father who awakened you is still awakening the world, and love has invited you to participate.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Awaken Your love in me.

> **Exhale:** Let it flow through me.
