## Day 11 — The Letters of Truth

> *You are a letter written by the hand of God.*

You have never existed outside the Father. Even in the years when you wandered, when God seemed distant or perhaps absent altogether, you remained within the One who surrounds every place and yet cannot himself be surrounded. The great mystery is not that you somehow found your way back to God, but that you could be held within God without knowing it.

This is the strange condition of forgetfulness. You can live surrounded by divine presence while feeling utterly alone, just as you can stand beneath the open sky and become so absorbed in your thoughts that you hardly notice it. The Father does not become absent when you lose awareness of him; it is your sight that has grown dim.

Yet God does not leave you in forgetfulness. What has been hidden begins to reveal itself as knowledge, like a book slowly opening before you. The words upon its pages are not empty marks but living signs, each carrying something of the truth from which it came.

Imagine your own life as one of those letters. You are not a meaningless stroke scattered across an indifferent universe, nor an accidental mark upon an empty page. Your existence belongs within a larger Word, written by the hand of the One from whom all things come.

A letter finds its meaning through relationship with the others around it. Alone it may seem small, but within a word it begins to speak, and within a sentence it participates in something larger than itself. So too your life becomes clearer when you stop trying to understand it in isolation and begin to see yourself within the great movement of divine love.

Perhaps this is why we sometimes struggle to understand ourselves. We stare at the single letter of our own life and wonder what it means, forgetting that we belong to a story larger than what we can presently see. There are sentences still unfolding and pages we have not yet read.

You do not need to decipher the entire book. It is enough to know the hand that writes it. As you come to know the Father, you may slowly discover that your life is not an empty symbol waiting desperately for meaning, but part of a living truth already being spoken.

Let yourself be read within that larger love. Your joys and wounds, your gifts and unfinished places, even the parts of your story you do not yet understand, can rest within the hands of the One who knows the whole. You are not meaningless ink upon the page; you are a letter through which the Father is still making himself known.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Write Your truth in me.

> **Exhale:** Let my life speak love.
