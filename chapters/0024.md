## Day 24 — The Fragrance of the Father

> *Love leaves its fragrance wherever it passes.*

The Father is sweet, and what flows from the Father carries that sweetness into the world. You may not always recognize the divine presence by sight or understand it through thought, but sometimes you know it the way you recognize a familiar fragrance. Something in you senses that love has passed near.

This fragrance appears in the fruit of a life. You notice it in patience that does not demand attention, kindness offered without calculation, forgiveness that loosens an old wound, or compassion that remains when it would be easier to turn away. These things carry the aroma of their Source.

You carry something of that fragrance too. You have come from the grace of the Father's countenance, and the life within you bears traces of the One from whom you came. Even when you feel ordinary, there are ways divine goodness can move through you that you may never fully see.

The Spirit recognizes this aroma and draws it homeward. What comes from the Father is attracted again toward the Father, like a breath returning to the air from which it came. Your deepest longing for goodness, beauty, unity, and love may be the fragrance of home awakening within you.

Yet division makes the heart cold. When you begin separating yourself from others through fear, contempt, envy, or the need to be right, something within you contracts. The warmth of love gives way to the chill of isolation, and you forget that your life belongs within a greater wholeness.

God comes into this coldness carrying the warmth of the Fullness. Divine love does not heal division by creating another division between the worthy and unworthy, the pure and impure, the enlightened and ignorant. Love moves toward unity, gathering what has become separated and warming what has grown cold.

Perhaps you can notice this movement in yourself. There are people toward whom your heart easily opens and others around whom it quickly closes. You do not need to manufacture affection, but you can allow the Father to soften the places where fear has taught you to withdraw from love.

Let your life carry the fragrance of the place from which you came. You do not need to announce it or convince anyone that it is there; fragrance does not argue for its presence. Simply remain near the Father until the sweetness of that presence begins to cling to everything you touch.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Fill me with Your sweetness.

> **Exhale:** Let me carry Your love.
