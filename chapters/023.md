## Day 23 — Do the Will of the Father

> *Do not return to what you have already outgrown.*

There are things you have laid down that will sometimes call you back. Old habits, old fears, and old ways of seeing yourself can remain strangely familiar even after you have begun to live differently. What is familiar can feel safe simply because you know its shape, even when you know it no longer gives you life.

So turn your attention inward and notice what you are reaching for. You do not need to become preoccupied with everything you have left behind, continually examining the past to make sure it is still there. Some things are best allowed to remain where you placed them.

Growth often requires learning not to feed what you have already released. A resentment weakens until you begin rehearsing it again; shame loses its voice until you return to the old accusations. Fear may knock at the door, but you are not required to prepare a room for it simply because it once lived there.

This does not mean you will never struggle with old patterns. Transformation rarely moves in a straight line, and returning briefly to something familiar does not erase the distance you have traveled. What matters is learning to recognize what is happening and turning again toward the life you have chosen.

Do not strengthen the obstacles that once stood between you and freedom. You have already seen enough light to know that some ways of living cannot carry you where you are going. Let that knowledge become wisdom rather than another reason to judge yourself.

The will of the Father is not a maze designed to make you afraid of taking a wrong turn. It is the movement of divine goodness drawing you toward what makes you more alive, more whole, and more capable of love. To do the Father's will is to participate in that movement.

You come from the Father, and something within you recognizes the direction of home. Beneath the noise of impulse, fear, and old conditioning, there is a quieter desire for what is true. Give that desire room to become strong.

You do not need to fight yesterday forever. Bless what taught you, release what no longer serves, and keep turning toward the life that is opening before you. The way forward is not found by staring at what you escaped, but by learning to walk freely toward the Father.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Strengthen what gives life.

> **Exhale:** Lead me toward You.
