## Day 27 — The Place of Rest

> *You are not emptied beyond the reach of fullness.*

There are seasons when you feel poured out. You give what you have to work, family, friendship, service, or simply the demands of getting through the day, and eventually you notice how little seems to remain. Even good things can leave you weary when you forget that you also need to receive.

Think of yourself as a vessel. A vessel is not defective because it becomes empty after pouring out what it carried. Its emptiness simply reveals that it was made both to give and to be filled again.

The Father knows this rhythm within you. God does not look upon your weariness as failure or demand that you somehow produce more from an empty place. The fullness of the Father moves toward what is lacking, filling again what has been spent.

This is the tenderness of the anointing. Christ comes to the troubled places with the oil of compassion, touching what has become dry and weary with the mercy of the Father. You are not restored by being scolded for your emptiness, but by receiving what you no longer have strength to provide for yourself.

Perhaps you have learned to distrust need. You may feel safer being the one who gives, helps, understands, or remains strong while others lean upon you. Receiving can feel vulnerable because it asks you to admit that you are not self-sufficient.

But nothing living is self-sufficient. The branch receives from the vine, the soil receives rain, and the lungs receive breath again and again. Your need to receive does not make you weak; it places you within the rhythm by which life itself is sustained.

The Father knows his plantings. You are not a forgotten seed struggling alone in barren ground, but something planted within the garden of divine care. The One who planted you also knows the conditions in which you can flourish.

And the deepest condition is rest. Paradise is not merely a distant reward at the end of life, but the place where the soul discovers that it no longer has to generate its own fullness. Let yourself become a vessel again, open to the compassion that fills what has been emptied and carries you gently into the Father's rest.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Fill what is empty.

> **Exhale:** Let me rest in You.
