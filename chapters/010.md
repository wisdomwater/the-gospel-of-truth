## Day 10 — Called by Name

> *The voice that calls you home knows your name.*

There is something deeply personal about being called by name. Across a crowded room, many voices may blend together, but when someone speaks your name, your attention turns almost without effort. You recognize that the voice is not simply making noise; someone is calling for you.

So it is with the Father. You are not merely one life among countless others, known only as part of some vast creation. The One from whom you came knows you, and the call that draws you home is spoken into the particular shape of your life.

For a long time you may not recognize the voice. Forgetfulness can leave you wandering through life as though you belong only to the identities the world has given you. You learn to call yourself by your work, your accomplishments, your failures, your wounds, your reputation, and the expectations others have placed upon you.

Then something within you hears another name. It may come through silence, beauty, love, grief, or an unexpected moment of clarity when the life you have constructed suddenly feels too small. You turn toward the voice because somewhere beneath all your borrowed identities, you recognize the One who is calling.

To be called is also to remember where you came from and where you are going. Like someone waking from intoxication, you slowly return to yourself and begin gathering what has been scattered. Things that once seemed desperately important loosen their grip, while quieter and more enduring things begin to matter again.

This does not mean discovering a secret identity hidden somewhere inside you, untouched by life. It means allowing yourself to be known by the Father more deeply than you have known yourself. Your truest name is received in relationship, shaped by the love of the One who called you into being.

And with that recognition comes rest. You no longer have to spend every moment inventing yourself or proving that your life deserves to exist. You can begin asking a gentler question: What would it mean today to live as someone who has already been called and already belongs?

Listen for that voice beneath the many voices competing for your attention. It does not call you toward fear or shame, but toward the One who knows where you began and where your journey leads. When you hear your name, turn toward the Father and let yourself come home.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** You call me by name.

> **Exhale:** I turn toward You.
