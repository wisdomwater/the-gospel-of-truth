## Day 9 — What Belongs to Us

> *What is truly yours has been waiting for you in God.*

There is a fullness in the Father that your heart recognizes even when your mind cannot explain it. You feel its absence as longing, as though some part of you knows there is more to life than what you can presently see or hold. This longing is not necessarily evidence that something has gone wrong; it may be the quiet pull of what belongs to you calling you home.

Ignorance feels like deficiency because you cannot receive what you do not yet recognize. You may be surrounded by love and still feel unloved, held by grace and still imagine yourself abandoned. The gift can be present while remaining hidden simply because you have not yet learned how to see it.

Knowledge begins to change this. As you awaken to the Father, you begin to recognize what has always been waiting for you there. You discover that the spiritual life is not about accumulating enough goodness to purchase something from God, but about receiving what has already been prepared in love.

There are parts of yourself you may have searched for in many places. You may have looked to achievement for your worth, to other people for your identity, or to certainty for your sense of safety. These things can offer something real, but they cannot give you what can only be received from the Source of your life.

In the Father you begin to recover what is yours. You find a belovedness that does not depend upon applause, a belonging that does not disappear when someone rejects you, and a life deeper than the roles by which you have learned to define yourself. You are not creating these things from nothing; you are drawing them from the One in whom they have always been held.

This return is not a retreat from the world. The more securely you receive what belongs to you in God, the less desperately you need the world to give it to you. You can work without asking achievement to tell you your worth, love without demanding another person complete you, and enter uncertainty without losing the ground beneath your feet.

The Father does not prepare these gifts reluctantly. They have their beginning in him and are given because you also have your beginning there. What you receive is not foreign to you; it is the life for which you were made.

So turn again toward the Father without fear that you will arrive empty-handed. The fullness you seek is already there, and what belongs to you has not been lost. As you come to know the One from whom you came, you may find yourself becoming more deeply who you have always been.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** All I need is held in You.

> **Exhale:** Restore what is mine.
