## Day 28 — The Will of the Father

> *Your beginning and your ending rest within God.*

There are depths within the Father that you cannot enter by thought alone. Before anything became visible, it rested there in silence, held within divine intention. Creation itself emerged from this hidden place, as the Word gave expression to what had been dwelling quietly within the heart of God.

Perhaps your own life is like this more often than you realize. Not everything needs to become clear immediately, and not every season requires visible progress. Some things grow first in silence, taking shape beneath the surface long before you can recognize what they are becoming.

You may want to know the will of God as though it were a map. You would like the next turn marked clearly, the destination identified, and some assurance that you will not make a mistake along the way. Uncertainty can make you anxious, especially when the choices before you seem important.

Yet the Father's will is deeper than a set of hidden instructions you must somehow discover. It is the movement of divine love carrying creation toward fullness, the quiet intention beneath all things that they should come to know their Source. You participate in that will whenever your life turns toward truth, mercy, wholeness, and love.

There will still be mystery. You will not always understand why one door opens while another closes, why some prayers seem answered and others disappear into silence. Trust does not require you to pretend that every event is exactly what God desired; it asks you to believe that no event can place you beyond the reach of divine love.

The Father knows beginnings and endings in ways you cannot. You see only the small stretch of road beneath your feet, while God holds the whole journey within a larger knowing. This does not make your choices meaningless; it allows you to make them without believing that everything depends upon your ability to see perfectly.

And where is all of this going? Toward recognition. What came forth from the Father is being drawn again toward the Father, awakening to the hidden Source from which its life began.

So you do not need to force tomorrow into clarity. Listen for the movement of love in the life before you today, and follow it as faithfully as you can. Your beginning came forth from the Father, your journey unfolds within the Father, and your ending is a return into the joy from which you came.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** My beginning is in You.

> **Exhale:** My ending is in You.
