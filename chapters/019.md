## Day 19 — The Path for Those Who Wander

> *The way home comes looking for those who are lost.*

You may imagine that finding God depends upon your ability to find the right path. When you wander, you assume the solution is to try harder, understand more, or somehow become worthy of returning. Yet Christ comes into the place where you are lost and becomes the path beneath your feet.

He comes revealing the Father who cannot be measured or contained. The invisible takes on a human face, and the heart of God is spoken through a human voice. Light enters ordinary flesh so that what seemed distant can be encountered near at hand.

Listen to what comes through that voice. It speaks understanding to the confused, mercy to those who have failed, salvation to those who feel trapped, and strength to those who have grown weary. These are not gifts reluctantly offered after you have proven yourself; they flow from the limitless sweetness of the Father.

Perhaps you have known another kind of religion. You may have encountered voices that relied upon punishment, shame, or fear to keep you moving in the right direction. Fear can certainly make a person move, but it rarely teaches the heart how to rest in love.

Christ loosens those chains. He does not deny that we lose our way or pretend that our choices carry no consequences, but he refuses to make terror the instrument of transformation. Knowledge of the Father begins to replace the fear that once kept you captive.

For the one who wanders, Christ becomes a path. For the one who does not understand, he becomes knowledge; for the one who keeps searching, discovery; for the trembling, support; and for the stained, cleansing. He meets each person not with a single demand, but with the grace their particular need requires.

You do not have to be ashamed of needing what he offers. There will be days when you are the wanderer, days when you are confused, and days when all you can do is tremble. Your need does not disqualify you from the path; it is often the very place where the path appears.

So begin where you are. You do not need to find your way back to some imagined place where you were more certain, more faithful, or more whole. Christ meets you here, and the next step home is already beneath your feet.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Meet me where I am.

> **Exhale:** Be my way home.
