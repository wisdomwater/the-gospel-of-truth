## Day 13 — From Division to Unity

> *Love gathers what fear has divided.*

You know what it is to feel divided within yourself. One part of you desires peace while another rehearses old wounds; one part trusts while another remains afraid. You may carry competing desires, unfinished griefs, and contradictions that make you wonder whether you will ever feel whole.

Division grows easily where we have forgotten the Father. When we lose sight of the Love that holds all things together, we begin separating the world into fragments: sacred and ordinary, worthy and unworthy, those who belong and those who do not. We defend our little territories because we have forgotten the larger unity in which all things have their life.

This incompleteness reveals itself through envy and strife. You compare your life with another and imagine that their abundance diminishes your own. You protect yourself against people you perceive as threats, forgetting that beneath all our differences we share the same hunger to be known, loved, and made whole.

The way back is not to erase every difference. Unity is not sameness, nor does love require the beautiful diversity of creation to disappear. The deeper unity comes when everything finds its place within the Father, like many streams discovering that they have always belonged to the same great sea.

Knowledge begins this healing. As you awaken to the Father, the boundaries built by fear become less absolute, and the scattered pieces of your life begin to find their center. You no longer need every part of yourself to compete for control because you are learning to rest within something larger than them all.

This is how light consumes darkness and life consumes death. Not through violence, but through presence. When light enters a room, darkness does not have to be dragged away; it simply discovers that it has nowhere left to remain.

Allow this same light to enter your divisions. You do not need to despise the frightened parts of yourself or force your contradictions into immediate resolution. Bring them gently into the presence of the Father and let love begin the slower work of gathering what has been scattered.

There is a wholeness deeper than the fragments you presently see. The Father is drawing all things toward unity, including the unfinished places within you. As you learn to see yourself and others within that larger love, what has been divided can slowly remember that it belongs together.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Gather what is scattered.

> **Exhale:** Make me whole in You.
