## Day 7 — The Quiet Teacher

> *Wisdom does not need to make itself loud.*

Christ comes among us as a teacher, quiet and at rest. He does not need to force himself upon those who gather around him, nor does he seem anxious to prove that he belongs there. He speaks from a deeper place, as one who knows the Father and is content to let truth carry its own weight.

Those who consider themselves wise may find such a teacher unsettling. We become attached to what we know, especially when our knowledge has become part of how we see ourselves. Sometimes we approach God less ready to listen than to have our understanding confirmed, testing whatever we hear against the boundaries we have already drawn.

Yet the wisdom of God has a way of slipping past these defenses. It is often received most freely by those who have stopped pretending to understand everything. There is a childlike openness that does not mean abandoning thought, but allowing wonder to remain where certainty has reached its limit.

These are the little ones who begin to recognize the Father's face. They learn slowly, becoming strong not because every mystery has been explained, but because they have discovered themselves to be known. They know the Father, and in the same movement they awaken to the astonishing truth that the Father knows them.

There is a difference between knowing about someone and allowing yourself to be known by them. You can spend years studying God while carefully protecting the hidden rooms of your own heart. Yet love invites something more intimate: to stand before the Father without disguise and discover that what is seen there is not rejected.

Within such a heart, a living book begins to open. Its words are not written merely on pages but into the life of the person who receives them. Truth moves from something you discuss into something you embody, quietly shaping the way you see, forgive, listen, and love.

Perhaps this is why Christ teaches from a place of rest. Truth does not need your anxiety to sustain it, and God does not require you to solve every mystery before you can belong. You can loosen your grip on the need to know everything and become a student again.

Come, then, with the openness of a child and the patience of one willing to learn. Let the quiet Teacher lead you beyond the boundaries of what you already understand. As you come to know the Father, allow yourself also to discover the deeper grace: you have been known all along.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Teach me in Your quiet.

> **Exhale:** Let me be known.
