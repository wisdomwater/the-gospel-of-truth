## Day 17 — The Nightmare

> *Morning reveals what fear made real in the night.*

You know how convincing a nightmare can be while you are inside it. You run from something you cannot see, fall from impossible heights, fight enemies who appear from nowhere, or find yourself trapped with no way out. Your body responds as though everything were real, because until you awaken, the dream is the only world you know.

Fear can shape waking life in much the same way. When you lose sight of the Father, confusion begins creating its own world around you. You imagine threats that have not yet come, rehearse disasters that may never happen, and defend yourself against dangers constructed as much from memory and uncertainty as from what is actually before you.

This does not make your fear foolish. A frightened heart experiences its shadows as real, just as the sleeping body races beneath the weight of a dream. Compassion begins when you stop condemning yourself for being afraid and become curious about what your fear has taught you to see.

Some dreams are shared by whole communities. We learn to fear one another, to divide the world into enemies and allies, to believe that there is not enough love, security, or belonging to go around. We can wound others while trying to protect ourselves, never realizing how much of the battle is being fought inside a world shaped by forgetfulness.

Yet the Father remains beyond the confusion. Fear does not alter God, and the shadows moving through your imagination cannot change the love in which you are held. The nightmare feels absolute only until another light begins to enter.

Awakening does not mean that every danger in life is imaginary. There is real suffering, real loss, and real reason at times to be afraid. But fear often adds another world on top of what is real, filling the unknown with phantoms and asking you to live as though the worst thing you can imagine has already happened.

Knowledge of the Father is like dawn entering that darkness. Slowly the shapes around you become clearer, and things that terrified you in the night begin to lose their power. You discover that you do not have to obey every frightened thought simply because it appeared within you.

So when fear surrounds you, wait for morning. Hold your conclusions gently and remember that darkness can distort the shapes of things. The Father is still present beyond what you can presently see, and the light is already moving toward the horizon.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Your light is rising.

> **Exhale:** Awaken me from fear.
