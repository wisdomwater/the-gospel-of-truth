## Day 25 — Turning Back

> *The moment you turn toward home, you discover home turning toward you.*

There is a hope within you that reaches toward something more complete than the life you presently know. At times it may be little more than a quiet intuition, a sense that beneath the shadows there is a light without darkness. You wait for that fullness even when you cannot clearly name what you are waiting for.

Waiting can make you aware of what is missing. You notice the unfinished places in yourself, the distance between the love you desire and the love you are able to give, or the gap between the world as it is and the world as you hope it might become. Deficiency can seem to be everywhere once you begin looking for it.

Yet do not confuse what is incomplete with the Father. The fullness of God is not diminished because you experience lack, nor does your brokenness reveal a brokenness in the Source from which you came. The depths of the Father remain inexhaustible even when you are standing in an empty place.

Sometimes all you can do from that place is turn. You may not know the whole path home or understand how everything within you will be restored. You simply recognize that the direction you have been traveling cannot give you life, and something in you begins to face another way.

This turning does not need to be dramatic. It may happen in a quiet decision to release an old resentment, tell the truth, ask for help, begin praying again, or stop running from something within yourself. A small movement toward the light can contain an entire change of direction.

And as you turn, you discover that you are not the only one moving. The Father is already present in the place of your deficiency, meeting you there with a fullness you could never create for yourself. What feels like your return to God may also be your awakening to the God who has already come near.

This is why falling does not have to become your final story. You can fall and be set upright again; you can wander and still turn toward home. The possibility of return remains because the love toward which you turn has not changed.

So do not wait until you feel complete before turning toward the Father. Bring the unfinished life you actually have, with its shadows, hopes, contradictions, and hunger. Turn as you are, and trust that the light toward which you face has already begun to shine upon you.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** I turn toward You.

> **Exhale:** Meet me with Your fullness.
