## Day 15 — When Truth Appears

> *Truth unsettles what cannot survive the light.*

When truth enters your life, it does not always arrive quietly. What has been carefully arranged within you may begin to shift, as though the shelves have been shaken and everything must be examined again. Some things are emptied, others filled, some restored to their proper place, and others discovered to have been broken for a long time.

This can feel like disorder, especially when you have grown accustomed to the way things were. Old explanations may stop satisfying you, familiar fears may lose their authority, and beliefs you once considered certain may suddenly seem too small. You may wonder whether you are losing your way when, in fact, you are beginning to see more clearly.

Error becomes troubled when knowledge approaches because it has nothing of its own on which to stand. It survives by remaining unquestioned, drawing strength from habits of thought repeated so often that they begin to feel like reality. When truth appears, what seemed solid can suddenly reveal itself as empty.

Do not be surprised if this awakening brings grief. There can be sorrow in discovering that something you trusted was shaped by fear, or that an image of yourself you carried for years was never entirely true. Even liberation can feel like loss when the prison has become familiar.

Yet truth does not come merely to dismantle. It comes to join you again to the Father. The Spirit moves within this revealing, drawing what has been scattered toward the mouth of God, where truth is spoken not as accusation but as communion.

You may be tempted to rebuild too quickly. When an old certainty falls away, the empty space can feel uncomfortable, and you may reach immediately for another answer to put in its place. But some spaces need to remain open for a while so that something deeper can emerge.

Let truth do its work without demanding that you understand every movement. Allow what is empty to be emptied and what is living to be filled. The disturbance you feel may be the sound of an inner world being rearranged around something more real.

When truth appears, you do not lose what is deepest in you. You lose what could never finally sustain you, and beneath it you discover the Father who has been there all along. Let the Spirit join you to that truth until what you know in your mind becomes something you can live from your heart.

### Breath Prayer

> **Inhale:** Reveal what is true.

> **Exhale:** Join me to Your heart.
